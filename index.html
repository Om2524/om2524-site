How it works

From one expert to every learner

Four steps turn scattered documents and one expert's experience into knowledge transfer that scales.

  1. 01

    Step 1

    Upload SOPs & docs

    Drop in manuals, SOPs, slide decks and recordings. Omicron reads and structures everything your expert already knows.

    • Document Parsing
    • Speech-to-Text
  2. 02

    Step 2

    AI captures the expert's voice & thinking

    A short session with your expert captures how they sound, how they explain, and how they reason through edge cases.

    • Voice Cloning
    • LLM Reasoning
  3. 03

    Step 3

    Generates narrated, interactive modules

    Documents become bite-sized modules narrated in the expert's voice, with visuals, checkpoints and scenarios.

    • LLM Reasoning
    • Text-to-Speech
    • Video Generation
  4. 04

    Step 4

    Voice tutor teaches and cross-questions learners

    Learners talk to the expert's voice tutor, ask follow-ups live, and get questioned back until the knowledge sticks.

    • Speech-to-Text
    • LLM Reasoning
    • Text-to-Speech

Capabilities

Everything it takes to transfer expertise

One platform, from capturing knowledge to proving it landed.

  • Expert Voice Cloning

    A faithful clone of your expert's voice and persona, so every lesson sounds like it came from them.

  • Live Voice Tutor

    Learners speak naturally and interrupt with questions. The tutor answers in the expert's style, in real time.

  • Doc-to-Course Engine

    SOPs, manuals and PDFs become structured, narrated modules in minutes, not months.

  • Multilingual Narration

    Deliver the same expert knowledge in the languages your teams and students actually speak.

  • Voice Assessments

    Spoken check-ins that test understanding, not memorisation, and score every answer.

  • Analytics Dashboard

    See who has learned what, where they struggle, and how ready each team is, all in one view.
